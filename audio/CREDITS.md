with no attribution required. The creators are credited anyway. They were brought over from Plastic Platoon
(`uberwulf-hub/armyMensRevenge`) and Race Track Mayhem (`uberwulf-hub/Race-Track-Mayhem`), whose credits
record the same sources.

## More free sounds

- **Kenney.nl**: CC0, no account needed. The best all-round source for game sounds.
- **OpenGameArt.org**: license varies per file; filter for CC0 (or CC-BY, which needs a credit line).
- **freesound.org**: license varies per sound; needs a free account. Avoid "NonCommercial" sounds.
- **Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod)**: great music, CC-BY 4.0, so it needs a credit line.
- Avoid putting raw files from Pixabay, Mixkit, Zapsplat or Sonniss bundles in the repo: their licenses allow
  using them in a game but not sharing the raw files.
