## New
- Added a touch joystick on phones and tablets: slide your left thumb to run, push up to jump, keep holding up for the double jump or flick up again to time it yourself
- Moved jumping to the left thumb, so you can run, jump and shoot at the same time; FIRE is now a big button on the right
- Added AJ: gold cards, and specials that talk robots into a nap, a latte bomb, a lullaby and a golden limo
- Added Allison: crochet hooks and knitting needles, and crocheted plushies (bunny, frog, octopus, bee, whale, dino) that pop into balls of yarn and tangle robots up
- Added Grandpa: swings his old guitar like a bat, twangs strings, slams the ground, throws a guitar boomerang, and drops a giant guitar from the sky
- Added Grandma: throws hearts, heals and shields the whole team, sends care packages, and turns robots into puppies that float away on heart balloons
- Added sleeping robots: they can't shoot and take extra damage

## Changed
- Seven heroes to pick from on the join screen; R (or SWAP) cycles through all of them
- Online games need everyone on this version
