## New
- Added a double jump: press jump again in the air, so blown-up ground never leaves you stuck
- Added sharper, more detailed graphics: outlined characters, shading, glowing visors and blinking robot lights
- Added soft lighting from explosions, lasers, lanterns and uranium
- Added snowy mountains and trees to Grumbot Valley, lanterns and timber to the Uranium Mines, lit-up buildings to the Plushie Factory, and searchlights and a castle wall to Liberty Fortress
- Added pebbles, grass tufts and flowers to the ground, and shadows under everyone's feet
- Fire is now Q and bomb is E, right next to WASD (the old keys still work)
- Swap brother is now R
